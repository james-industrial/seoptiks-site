FAQs

Common questions

What types of leads will I get?

Depending on the plan you choose, you'll receive leads identifying the companies visiting your website along with relevant contact information. Our advanced plan includes detailed contact-level data — names, emails, direct dials, and LinkedIn profiles — for specific individuals from those companies.

How does SEOptiks identify visitors?

Our visitor tracking software uses high-quality third-party data sources that compile billions of unique data points to create a comprehensive view of the companies and individuals using your website. We match IP addresses and behavioural signals to firmographic databases to return accurate, actionable results.

How quickly will I see my first lead?

Often within five minutes of installation — it depends on your current traffic levels. If you have visitors right now, we can likely identify them immediately. For lower-traffic sites, combining SEOptiks with paid advertising or SEO will accelerate results.

Can I track multiple websites under one account?

Yes. Each unique website requires its own subscription for tracking and data, but you can manage them all from a single login — keeping your reporting clean and centralised.

Are subscriptions monthly or annual?

We offer flexible monthly subscriptions with no long-term commitment. Cancel at any time — your access continues until the end of your billing period.

How accurate is the data?

We invest in the highest-quality data available. Rather than cutting costs on data quality, we believe accuracy is what drives real sales results. The best way to judge for yourself is to book a free demo — we'll show you live results on your own site.

Is the software GDPR compliant?

Yes. SEOptiks is built with GDPR and UK data protection regulations in mind. We identify companies at a business level and handle all data in a compliant manner. If you have specific compliance questions, reach out to our team directly.
